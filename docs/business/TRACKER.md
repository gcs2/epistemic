## Proposed first test card — not launched

- Hypothesis: a reachable buyer has recurring reporting work worth paying to improve.
- Candidate audience: small agencies; replace if founder access favors another segment.
- Proposed batch: five qualified discovery conversations, reviewed as qualitative learning.
- Offer/message version: drafts in [customers and offers](CUSTOMERS_AND_OFFERS.md) and [sales and marketing](SALES_AND_MARKETING.md).
- Owner, actual audience, review date, time cap, and cash cap: unset.
- Before launch: choose qualification criteria and the evidence threshold for offering a paid pilot.
- Observe: recent workflow examples, existing alternatives, urgency, budget authority, data access, objections, and willingness to take a concrete paid next step.
- Revise/stop: no recurring problem, inadequate safe access, no delivery competence, insufficient value, or budget/time cap reached. Review mixed evidence without quietly widening the target market.
- Result: not run.

## Ongoing review

While active, review weekly and after every pilot. Record counts with denominators and time windows, qualified opportunities and their next action, actual versus estimated labor/costs, collected cash and obligations, customer outcomes, incidents, and research capacity.

Select one immediate bottleneck and assign one next action with owner and review date. Stop adding sales volume if delivery quality or capacity is failing. Stop funding engine additions that lack a demonstrated need.
