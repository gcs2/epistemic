# Self-sustaining business initiative

**Initiative:** BIZ-001

**Established:** 2026-09-05

**Stage:** Planning; no commercial validation recorded in this repository.

Build a business that creates measurable customer value, pays for its full delivery costs, and can eventually fund SES research. Revenue is a means of sustaining the mission, not evidence that the scientific method works.

The initial working strategy is **a narrow, paid service first; reusable products only after repeated demand**. This is a proposal to test, not a proven route to income. A service can use ordinary engineering and research alongside SES; it need not depend on an unvalidated engine. Public brand, customer segment, price, budget, and launch timing remain undecided.

## Stages and evidence gates

1. **Choose a reachable problem.** Identify a recurring task, a budget owner, an existing workaround, and a safe way to observe success. Prefer customer access over an impressive but inaccessible market.
2. **Test demand.** Document concrete customer accounts and attempt a bounded paid offer. Interest, compliments, and AI-generated market arguments are not purchases.
3. **Deliver one paid pilot.** Record scope, baseline, acceptance criteria, time, costs, cash collection, and customer outcome. A sale without useful delivery does not pass this gate.
4. **Test repeatability.** Look for repeat purchases, referrals, multiple customers with materially similar needs, and positive contribution after counting labor and acquisition. One successful engagement is insufficient to claim a scalable business.
5. **Productize selectively.** Automate repeated delivery steps first. Offer subscriptions only when customers have an ongoing need and support economics work; do not force recurring billing onto a one-time problem.
6. **Fund research from genuine surplus.** Preserve delivery obligations and a chosen cash reserve before allocating money to compute. Scale only when the relevant bottleneck and marginal benefit are demonstrated.

Passage is evidence-based, not automatic after a calendar deadline. Gate thresholds and spending caps must be agreed before their test; revise them openly rather than moving the goalposts after disappointing results.

## Definition of self-sustaining

Collected customer revenue covers delivery, acquisition, support, agreed founder compensation, overhead, and applicable obligations over a declared review period, while maintaining a chosen reserve and funding an explicit research allocation. Define the period, reserve, and allocation in the tracker before claiming success.

Profitability, customer benefit, scientific validity, and public benefit are separate scorecards. A business can succeed while adding no novel science; a valid scientific result can have no paying market.

## Ownership and maintenance

The founder is the decision sponsor; operational owners and time commitments remain unassigned until accepted. No recurring automation is created by this plan.

[TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md) is the only business execution ledger. Root [STATUS.md](../../STATUS.md) summarizes project state and links here; [FINDINGS.md](../../FINDINGS.md) remains the scientific findings ledger. Update this compendium when decisions change, not by creating competing roadmaps.

Review weekly while actively operating and after each completed pilot. Record dated decisions, disconfirming evidence, actual costs, and next actions. Keep private customer records outside this public repository; public templates contain no live contacts or confidential data.
