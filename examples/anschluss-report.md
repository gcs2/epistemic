## Registered challenges

- **inquiry antecedent:** A permanent end to expansion conflicts with Hitler's repeatedly stated ideological objectives; an illness, restraint mechanism, or different leader may be a more coherent intervention.
- **hitler_regime_retains:** Economic consolidation requires reducing or redirecting the mobilization system on which Nazi legitimacy and elite competition increasingly depended.
- **all hypotheses:** The inquiry has not yet modeled Soviet, Italian, Japanese, Polish, Czechoslovak, or colonial dynamics as endogenous responses.

## Declared limitations

- All numerical priors and likelihoods are illustrative elicitation values, not a completed scholarly estimate.
- The antecedent changes a central feature of Hitler's ideology and therefore has substantial actor-coherence uncertainty.
- The three hypotheses compress many possible successions, coups, negotiated settlements, and conflicts.
- No source-provenance graph or independently calibrated expert panel has yet populated this example.

## Interpretation discipline

The sensitivity intervals reflect only the perturbations declared in this inquiry. They do not include unknown hypotheses, fabricated evidence, model misspecification, or unrecorded source dependence. Review the inputs before relying on the result.
