## Safety and reversibility

- **Known scoring hazard:** 4 negative impact cell(s) have confidence below 1.0. Action protocol 0.1 moves those harms toward zero and can understate downside; the ranking is exploratory and robust certification is disabled.
- All options are declared reversible and below the high-risk category; this declaration still requires human verification.
- **Stop rule:** Stop if a reported privacy incident occurs.
- **Stop rule:** Stop if staff overtime exceeds the approved cap.
- **Appeal:** Participants and staff can request review through the program manager; reasons and disposition are logged.
- **Affected-party review:** A resident advisory group reviews language, location, privacy, and burden before launch.

## Learning contract

- **Primary metric:** First-time program attendance per staff hour
- **Measurement:** Compare the six-week pilot with the prior six-week baseline and report results by outreach channel and available demographic groups.
- **Duration:** Six weeks
- **Success rule:** At least 15% improvement over baseline without a privacy incident, excessive overtime, or reduced participation from an observed underserved group.

## Decision discipline

Do not scale from this report alone. Verify the factual inputs, invite affected parties to challenge the value weights and impact scores, run the bounded pilot, record adverse effects, and update or abandon the model after observation.
