## Adaptive learning contract

- **Review date:** Six weeks after launch
- **Measurement:** Compare the six-week pilot with the prior six-week baseline and report intended and adverse outcomes by outreach channel and available stakeholder group.
- **Success rule:** At least 15% improvement in first-time attendance per staff hour without a stop-rule event or observed participation decline for underserved residents.
- **Trigger:** Review weekly if staff overtime exceeds 80% of the approved cap.
- **Trigger:** Re-estimate the access scenario if digital response is below half the expected rate after two weeks.
- **Stop rule:** Stop if a reported privacy incident occurs.
- **Stop rule:** Stop if staff overtime exceeds the approved cap.
- **Stop rule:** Stop if observed underserved-resident participation declines for two consecutive measurement periods.
- **Appeal:** Participants and staff can request review through the program manager; reasons and disposition are logged.
- **Affected-party review:** A resident advisory group reviews language, location, privacy, burden, and the consequence ranges before launch.

## Interpretation boundary

The intervals are sensitivity distributions induced by declared triangular ranges, probability concentrations, value ranges, and uncertainty groups. They are not frequentist confidence intervals and do not include unimagined states. Review the open deficits and challenge coverage before any pilot, and require independent authority for any real action.
