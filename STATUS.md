## What is established within synthetic scope

1. Correlated repetition creates overconfidence when counted as independent evidence.
2. Overcorrecting dependence discards real information when observations are independent.
3. Noisy dependence estimates can recover much of the available benefit under a favorable error model.
4. Hidden source-family recovery is a precision–recall problem, not a generic deduplication task.
5. Unconditional union of citation and text-similarity edges can create catastrophic false mergers through transitive closure.
6. Ordinary accuracy can hide large differences in probability quality and confidently wrong behavior.
7. In the Experiment 003A generator, agreement among declared views did not protect against a shared omitted tail.
8. Multiplying low-confidence negative impacts by confidence can understate harms and increase tail exposure.
9. The registered robustness gate reduced tail exposure but failed its joint protection criterion because deferral imposed excessive regret.

## What is not established

- Accurate recovery of real source ancestry.
- Reliable likelihood elicitation from natural evidence.
- Superiority of SES over expert panels, forecasting teams, or other agent workflows.
- Cross-disciplinary translation quality.
- Causal validity or open-world hypothesis discovery.
- Human legitimacy, usability, or decision improvement.
- Evidence that Action Protocol 0.2 outperforms simpler decision methods.

## Completed experiments

- Experiment 001: 375,000 trials, 150 cells, all six preregistered hypotheses supported.
- Experiment 002A: 17,280 generated cases, 108 cells, six of eight preregistered hypotheses supported; two favored-method hypotheses failed and were retained.
- Experiment 003A: 96,000 synthetic decision cases, 96 cells, four of seven preregistered hypotheses supported; H5–H7 were not supported and a post-run H5 classification correction is documented.

Experiments 001, 002A, and 003A have byte-identical deterministic replications and result manifests. Experiment 003A's frozen inputs also verify independently through `FROZEN.sha256`.

## Executable action protocols

Action Protocol 0.1 is a legacy reproduction path. Experiment 003A exposed its signed-confidence defect. Its analyzer disables robust certification whenever a negative impact has confidence below 1.0, but its ranking still reproduces the challenged rule. Do not use it for new live decisions.

Action Protocol 0.2, Bounded Decision Assurance, is now executable. It uses explicit consequence bounds, uncertain value weights, simple declared dependence groups, separate evidence quality, stakeholder constraints, challenge coverage, assurance deficits, lower-tail outcomes, and an adaptive learning contract. Its invariance tests establish that changing an evidence-quality label does not numerically shrink signed consequences. This is implementation evidence only; it does not establish better real decisions.

## Next decisive experiment

The 003B investigation slice has completed two public development runs, each
120 cases x five regimes x six policies. The second reuses cases at budget two
after the first exposed a full-menu comparison limitation. This is development,
not protected confirmation. See [interpretation](experiments/003b-bounded-decision-assurance/DEVELOPMENT_INTERPRETATION.md).
One-step planning remains the default. More lookahead has not established an
advantage over it; misleading likelihoods and absent hypotheses remain major failures.

Audit correction: Action 0.2's tail/constraint statistics refer to expected-score
sensitivity, not realized-outcome risk. Its learning contract remains declarative.
Investigation 0.1 separately computes exact discrete outcome tails and updates
beliefs through explicitly costed observations. It assumes supplied hypotheses
and likelihoods; it does not discover omitted mechanisms.

Experiment 002B will use an adjudicated real corpus, beginning with published systematic-review citation matrices. It will compare citation, text, metadata, semantic, human, and constrained-hybrid methods, with false-merger rate and downstream proper scoring as primary outcomes.

Until 002B succeeds, inferred dependence should be represented as a range or reviewed manually rather than treated as a precise automatic weight.

Experiment 003B must now compare Action 0.2 with expected utility, Action 0.1, minimax regret, SMAA-like stochastic acceptability, RDM-like stress testing, and evidence-failure auditing. It must include active stakeholder conflict, protected confirmation cases, matched deferral and process costs, and component ablations. A real pilot additionally requires a genuine decision owner, affected-party review, a recorded baseline process, ethics and privacy review where applicable, and prospectively frozen outcomes.

Deep research DR-001 supplies the rationale for the implemented architectural reform. The comparative Experiment 003B remains a design draft, so the method's efficacy and novelty remain candidate claims.
